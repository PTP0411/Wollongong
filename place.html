Place Recommendations in Wollongong, Sydney, and Brisbane

I believe these cities will offer you a lot of amazing experience. From food, scenery, adventures. animals, you will not be bored here!

Wollongong

Wollongong, a coastal city with plenty of beautiful beaches and nature spots, is perfect for those looking to unwind or explore the outdoors.

  • Wollongong Botanic Garden - This botanical garden is right next to Kooloobong. Great for picnics or a relaxing day out.
    Location: 40 Murphys Ave, Keiraville NSW 2500, Australia
  • North Wollongong Beach - Perfect for a swim, surf, or a walk along the coast.
    Location: 5 Cliff Rd, North Wollongong, NSW, AU.
  • Symbio Wildlife Park - See a variety of Australian animals and enjoy a hands-on experience. (You can pet kangaroos and wallabys there)
    Location: 7/11 Lawrence Hargrave Dr, Helensburgh NSW 2508, Australia
  • Sea Cliff Bridge - The 665 metre long Sea Cliff Bridge is a highlight along the Grand Pacific Drive. A drive which takes in 140 kilometres worth of coastal scenery from the Royal National Park, Loftus down to Nowra.
    Location: Cliffton, NSW, Australia.

Sydney

Sydney, Australia's largest city, offers a mix of iconic landmarks, trendy neighborhoods, and vibrant culture.

  • Sydney Opera House - One of the most iconic landmarks in the world. Don't miss the chance to explore its architecture and attend a performance.
    Location: Bennelong Point, Sydney
  • Bondi-Coogee walk - A popular beach for swimming, surfing, and people-watching.
    Location: Bondi Beach, Sydney
  • Royal Botanic Garden - Beautiful gardens with stunning views of the Sydney Opera House and Harbour Bridge.
    Location: Mrs Macquaries Road, Sydney
  • Blue Mountains - An amazing grand canyon. I was lucky that IFSA took us there. If not, you can hop on a train to go there!
    Location: NSW, Australia

Brisbane

Brisbane, known for its warm climate and outdoor lifestyle, has something for everyone—whether it's the lively city center or scenic riverfront spots.

  • South Bank Parklands - A vibrant area along the Brisbane River with gardens, restaurants, and cultural attractions.
    Location: South Bank, Brisbane
  • Mount Coot-tha - Offering panoramic views of the city and surrounding nature. Great for a hike or a scenic drive.
    Location: Mount Coot-tha, Brisbane
  • Brisbane River Cruise - A relaxing way to explore the city, with options for lunch or sunset cruises.
    Location: Various departure points along the Brisbane River
